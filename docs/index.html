PARTY GAMES

Top-down party games for you and up to seven friends, wherever they are. One of you makes a room and the rest join with its code.

Party Games runs on computers: Windows, Mac and Linux. Send yourself this page and download it there.

Download

For Windows, macOS and Linux

Or get it for another system, or use the launcher, which keeps it up to date.

Getting playingEveryone needs the same version: the newest is always here.

1

Download it

Unzip it and open it. No account, no installer. What to click on your system.

2

Make a room

One of you opens the game: your room's code is at the top of the lobby. Everyone else presses Join Game and types it in.

3

Spin the wheel

Pull the lever by the wheel and let it pick, or press V and vote. Then try not to fall in the lava.

What's on the wheelFive slices. Each match ends on the podium.

TNT Games

Four games back to back on one scoreboard, or pick just one on the vote card.

  • TNT Run: every block you step on falls a moment later. Last one up wins.
  • Bow Spleef: your arrows burn the floor out. Shoot it out from under people.
  • TNT Tag: hand the TNT on before its fuse runs out.
  • TNT Wizards: two houses, three towers, four schools of magic.

The Quiver

First person, one arrow a life, and it kills outright. Fetch it back, or punch your way out. Three arenas.

Obstacles

A game show. Race the course: only the first few through each round go on, and the final decides it.

Party Games

Five quick minigames out of twelve, with the rules on a card before each. Points for where you come.

Zombies

Everyone together against waves of monsters. Spend your gold on doors, weapons and upgrades, and revive each other.

DownloadThe newest version, for each system. These links never go stale.

Windows

  1. Unzip PartyGames-Windows.zip.
  2. Run Party Games.exe.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Only the first time.
DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS

macOS

  1. Unzip PartyGames-macOS.zip (double-click it).
  2. Open Party Games. Drag it into Applications first if you like.
  3. It's signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any app. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
DOWNLOAD FOR MACOS

Linux

  1. Unzip PartyGames-Linux.zip.
  2. Run party_games.x86_64. If it won't start, make it runnable first: chmod +x party_games.x86_64.
  3. For 64-bit PCs (x86_64), on any recent desktop distribution.
